## ✨ Features

### 🧿 ESP & Visual

- **Player ESP** – Display player locations during gameplay
- **Skeletons** – Render skeletal player structures
- **Player Head & Direction** – Display head position and facing direction
- **Player Info** – Show player names and distance
- **Snaplines** – Draw configurable lines toward players
- **Hide Teammates** – Exclude teammates and Hunters from the display

### 🎯 Combat

- **Aim Assistance** – Adjustable target selection system
- **Visibility Check** – Determine if a target is currently visible
- **Aim Bone** – Choose between Head, Neck, or Body
- **FOV Circle** – Set the desired targeting radius
- **Smoothing** – Adjust aiming transition speed
- **Aim Key** – Set a custom activation key

### ⚡ Movement & Host Options

- **Teleport (HOST ONLY)** – Instantly change your current position
- **Speed Control (HOST ONLY)** – Modify movement speed
- **Spinbot** – Configure player rotation behavior

### 🛡️ Gameplay

- **God Mode** – Prevent incoming damage
- **Auto Paint** – Automate paint spraying and camouflage
- **Anti-Kick** – Reduce unwanted lobby disconnections
- **Anti-Buried** – Help prevent getting trapped inside terrain

### 🛠️ Utility

- **No Gun Cooldown** – Remove the delay between weapon shots
- **FOV Changer** – Customize the field of view
- **Chat Automation** – Send automated messages at configurable intervals
- **Menu Toggle** – Open the interface with `INSERT` or `F1`
